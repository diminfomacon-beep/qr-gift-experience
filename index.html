A Standard of Care

Composed to a considered length

“Each experience is composed to a considered length, so your message is watched, heard, and felt in full. The Video includes up to three minutes — the length most recipients treasure and rewatch. Should your message run longer, we will gladly extend your experience, minute by minute, through our Enhancements.”

Measured before it is made

Every file's length, word count, and photo count is checked on arrival, before design begins — so there are never surprises for you, or for us, after the work is done.

Preserved, on purpose

Every page carries a preservation term — 12 months, 24 months, or 5 years for the Legacy Vault. Renewal is $12 per year, always offered before expiry, so no memory ever disappears without warning.

One revision, with compliments

Each experience includes one light revision. Further rounds are available as an Enhancement, at $15 — so your gift can be perfected without the scope quietly growing.
